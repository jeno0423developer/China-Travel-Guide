Top Destinations

Explore China's most iconic cities — handpicked attractions and local travel tips for foreign visitors

Beijing

Imperial history meets modernity

Beijing

Capital city where 500+ years of imperial history unfold. Walk the Great Wall, explore the Forbidden City, and experience authentic Peking duck cuisine.

History Culture Landmarks
Must-See Attractions (via Dianping)
  • 故宫博物院 (Forbidden City) — China's imperial palace, a UNESCO site
  • 八达岭长城 (Badaling Great Wall) — Best-preserved section, cable car available
  • 颐和园 (Summer Palace) — Royal garden with Kunming Lake
  • 天坛公园 (Temple of Heaven) — Iconic circular hall for imperial sacrifices
  • 南锣鼓巷 (Nanluoguxiang) — Historic hutong alley with shops & cafes
Getting Around
  • Metro (地铁): Most convenient — covers all major sights. Buy a Beijing Transit Card or use Alipay/WeChat QR code.
  • Bus (公交): Routes 1-99 cover central areas; number 877 goes to Badaling Great Wall.
  • Taxi/Didi (打车): Widely available; use Didi app for English-friendly rides. Avoid yellow-metered cabs at night.
  • Great Wall Trip: Take S2 train from Beijing North Station to Badaling (¥25, 70 min) or join a hotel-organized day tour.

Shanghai

China's global financial hub

Shanghai

The "Pearl of the Orient" — a dazzling blend of colonial architecture, futuristic skyscrapers, and world-class dining along the Huangpu River.

Modern Shopping Nightlife
Must-See Attractions (via Dianping)
  • 外滩 (The Bund) — Colonial-era skyline, best at night with lights
  • 东方明珠 (Oriental Pearl Tower) — Observation deck & revolving restaurant
  • 豫园 (Yu Garden) — Classical Chinese garden near the Old City
  • 上海迪士尼乐园 (Disneyland) — Resort in Pudong, full day needed
  • 田子坊 (Tianzifang) — Artsy lanes with boutiques and craft shops
Getting Around
  • Metro (地铁): Extensive network, very efficient. Single ride ¥3-10. Download "Metro Man" app.
  • Ferry (轮渡): Cross Huangpu River between Puxi and Pudong (¥2, scenic views).
  • Bus (公交): Useful for French Concession area; routes 20, 37 cover Bund.
  • Taxi/Didi: Abundant; Didi app works well. Tolls apply to cross tunnels to Pudong.

Xi'an

Ancient capital of China

Xi'an

The starting point of the Silk Road — famous for the Terracotta Army, ancient city walls you can bike around, and vibrant Muslim food streets.

History Archeology Silk Road
Must-See Attractions (via Dianping)
  • 秦始皇兵马俑博物馆 (Terracotta Army) — World-famous, arrive early to avoid crowds
  • 大雁塔 (Giant Wild Goose Pagoda) — Tang dynasty Buddhist pagoda
  • 西安城墙 (Ancient City Wall) — China's best-preserved; rent a bike to cycle the full circle
  • 回民街 (Muslim Quarter / Huimin Street) — Famous for roujiamo, yangrou paomo, and skewered lamb
  • 大唐不夜城 (Tang Dynasty Night City) — Walking street with light shows & performances
Getting Around
  • Metro (地铁): Line 2 & 3 cover main areas; ¥2-7 per ride.
  • To Terracotta Army: Take Metro Line 1 to "Banpo" station, then transfer to Bus 5 (30 min). Or take direct tourist bus from Xi'an Train Station.
  • City Wall Bike: Rent a bicycle at any of the 4 wall gates (¥45/day) for a 2-hour ride around the perimeter.
  • Train from Beijing: High-speed rail takes ~4.5 hours (¥553 first class).

Guilin

Yangshuo — China's most beautiful landscape

Guilin / Yangshuo

"桂林山水甲天下" — Cruise the Li River through limestone karst mountains, then explore Yangshuo's riverside streets and rural cycling routes.

Nature Scenic Relaxation
Must-See Attractions (via Dianping)
  • 漓江游船 (Li River Cruise) — Guilin→Yangshuo, 4-hour boat ride through karst peaks (¥215+)
  • 阳朔西街 (Yangshuo West Street) — Pedestrian street with cafes, bars & souvenir shops
  • 遇龙河竹筏 (Yulong River Bamboo Raft) — Peaceful raft ride near Yangshuo (¥160-260/person)
  • 龙脊梯田 (Longji Rice Terraces) — Dramatic layered terraces, best Sept-Nov after harvest
  • 十里画廊 (Ten Mile Gallery) — Scenic cycling route from Yangshuo, hire a bike (¥10/day)
Getting Around
  • To Yangshuo: Bus from Guilin Station (¥25, 1.5h) or private car (¥150, 1h). Direct train from Guilin to Yangshuo also available.
  • River Cruise: Book through your hotel or online. Sit on the right side for better views. Bring sunscreen & a hat.
  • In Yangshuo: Everything is walkable. Rent electric bikes (¥30/day) for exploring nearby villages.
  • To Longji: Hire a private car (¥400 round trip, 3h each way) or join a tour bus from Guilin.

Chengdu

Home of the giant pandas

Chengdu

The laid-back capital of Sichuan — meet giant pandas in their natural habitat, explore ancient tea houses, and brave the world's spiciest cuisine.

Wildlife Food Culture
Must-See Attractions (via Dianping)
  • 大熊猫繁育研究基地 (Panda Breeding Base) — See pandas up close, arrive before 9am for active pandas
  • 宽窄巷子 (Kuan Zhai Alleys) — Traditional teahouses, Sichuan snacks, and cultural performances
  • 锦里古街 (Jinli Ancient Street) — Walking street near Wuhou Temple, good for souvenirs
  • 武侯祠博物馆 (Wuhou Temple) — Three Kingdoms era memorial, oldest in China
  • 春熙路 (Chunxi Road) — Main shopping area, try local snacks like stinky tofu and dragon's beard candy
Getting Around
  • Metro (地铁): Clean, efficient; Lines 1, 2, 3 cover main areas. ¥2-9 per ride.
  • To Panda Base: Bus 198 from Chunxi Road or take a taxi (~¥40, 30 min). Opens 7:30am — go early!
  • To Dujiangyan / Wolong: High-speed train from Chengdu West (¥10, 30 min) for Dujiangyan panda area.
  • Food Tip: Ask for "不辣" (not spicy) if you can't handle Sichuan chili. Try mapo tofu and hot pot at lunchtime.

Hong Kong

Asia's world city

Hong Kong

The former British colony — a vertical city of skyscrapers, street food, and world-class shopping on both sides of Victoria Harbour.

Shopping Cuisine Nightlife
Must-See Attractions (via Dianping)
  • 维多利亚港 (Victoria Harbour) — Take the Star Ferry (HK$6.5) for iconic skyline views
  • 太平山顶 (Victoria Peak) — Take the historic Peak Tram for panoramic views (HK$36 round trip)
  • 尖沙咀 (Tsim Sha Tsui) — Waterfront promenade, Cultural Centre, Avenue of Stars
  • 香港迪士尼乐园 (Disneyland) — On Lantau Island, MTR Disney Resort line
  • 尖沙咀天星码头 (TST Star Ferry Pier) — Watch the nightly "Symphony of Lights" at 8pm
Getting Around
  • MTR (港铁): World-class subway, covers everything. Buy an Octopus Card (HK$100 deposit) or use contactless credit card.
  • Star Ferry (天星小轮): The cheapest harbor cruise — Central ↔ TST (HK$2.5-6.5).
  • Tram (叮叮车): Vintage double-decker trams on Hong Kong Island (HK$2.6, great for sightseeing).
  • Peak Tram (山顶缆车): Central → Victoria Peak; cheaper to go up at opening (7am) to avoid queues.

Guiyang

Summer paradise in southwest China

Guiyang Featured

China's "Summer Capital" — cool mountains, dramatic waterfalls, and rich ethnic minority culture in Guizhou province.

Nature Waterfalls Ethnic Culture
Must-See Attractions (via Dianping)
  • 黄果树大瀑布 (Huangguoshu Waterfall) — China's largest waterfall, spectacular June-Oct
  • 青岩古镇 (Qingyan Ancient Town) — Ming dynasty fortress, 600+ years old, ethnic Miao culture
  • 甲秀楼 (Jiaxiu Tower) — Iconic wooden tower over the Nanming River
  • 小七孔 (Xiaoqikong Karst) — "Super aquarium of the karst world," turquoise water & stone arches
  • 西江千户苗寨 (Xijiang Miao Village) — World's largest Miao settlement, 1,288 households
Getting Around
  • To Huangguoshu: High-speed train from Guiyang to Anshun West (¥47, 35 min), then bus to waterfall (¥20, 1h). Full tour ¥300+.
  • To Qingyan: Bus 210 from Guiyang (¥2, 40 min) — most accessible attraction from the city.
  • To Xijiang: Train from Guiyang to Kaili (¥70, 2h), then minibus to village (¥25, 40 min). Stay overnight.
  • In Guiyang: City center is walkable; use Didi for longer trips. Try local sour soup fish (酸汤鱼)!
